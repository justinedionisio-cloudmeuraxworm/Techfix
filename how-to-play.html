📋 Step-by-Step Tutorial
1
🎯 Select a Mission

Choose a troubleshooting mission from the main menu.

2
🔍 Inspect Components

Examine computer components to gather evidence.

3
🛠️ Use Troubleshooting Tools

Use tools such as a screwdriver, flashlight, multimeter, and PSU tester to inspect hardware.

4
🧠 Analyze the Evidence

Review inspection results and identify the most likely cause of the problem.

5
✅ Submit Your Diagnosis

Select the most likely cause and submit it for evaluation — the system provides educational feedback automatically.
